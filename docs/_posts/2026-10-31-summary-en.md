### [Intel shares plunge ~28% on weak Q4 guidance, government stake down $12B](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/30/intels-stock-crashes-knocking-12b-from-governments-stake/) ⭐️ 7.5/10

Intel shares fell about 28% on Friday after the chipmaker guided fourth-quarter revenue to $15–16 billion, below the roughly $17 billion analysts expected, per TechCrunch. The selloff cut the value of the U.S. government&\#x27;s stake by $12 billion, a figure TechCrunch attributes to CNBC. CEO Lip-Bu Tan said customers are signaling a PC slowdown driven by memory prices and availability, even as data-center revenue grew 78% year over year.

rss · TechCrunch AI · Oct 30, 21:54

**「Why it matters」** Intel&\#x27;s guidance ties a PC demand slump to AI-driven memory costs, a squeeze that hits enterprise IT budgets and system prices. The drop also weighs on the government&\#x27;s 10% holding and on Intel&\#x27;s effort to fill fabs and win external foundry customers against TSMC.

**「Key figures」** Intel guided Q4 gross margin to 37% from 43% and said heavy investment means it expects no positive cash flow. The stock remains up more than 70% for the year. One new Arizona fab is operational; the next is not expected online until 2028.

### [Privacy-Focused AI Assistant Ollie to Shut Down November 6](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/30/ollie-a-privacy-focused-ai-assistant-is-shutting-down/) ⭐️ 7.2/10

Ollie, a privacy-focused AI assistant for families, will shut down on November 6, CEO Bill Lennon announced on X. The startup had raised a $7.5 million seed round led by Khosla Ventures and offered a subscription service connected to users’ email and calendars. Lennon said competition from Muse, Instinct and others made it harder to stand out, grow and raise capital; customers will receive data-export and refund instructions in the coming days.

rss · TechCrunch AI · Oct 30, 22:56

**「Impact」** The closure affects Ollie subscribers who used the assistant for tasks including appointment booking, meal planning and bill payments. Users will need to watch for export and refund instructions before the service goes offline.

**「Details」** Ollie disclosed its $7.5 million seed round in September and said it had achieved SOC 2 privacy compliance. Lennon did not provide a specific refund timeline in the announcement.

### [China TV Brand Shipments Fall 4.3% in Q3 2026; RUNTO Forecasts 17-Year Low for Full Year](https://www.ithome.com/1/020/465.htm) ⭐️ 7.3/10

RUNTO Technology \(洛图科技\) data reported by IT Home on 31 October 2026 shows mainland China TV brand shipments of 6.960 million units in Q3 2026, down 4.3% year on year. Cumulative shipments for the first three quarters were 21.907 million units, down 8.3% YoY. RUNTO forecasts full-year 2026 shipments of 30.360 million units, down 7.7% YoY — a projection the firm says would mark the smallest annual market in at least 17 years, since 2010. Per the report, this is the sixth consecutive quarter of YoY decline, following two quarters of growth after the national subsidy programme began in Q4 2024.

rss · IT Home industry reports \(strict trend filter\) · Oct 31, 05:28

**「Share concentrates among top eight brands」** According to the report, the top eight brands — Hisense, Skyworth, Xiaomi, TCL, Changhong, Haier, Konka and Huawei, including sub-brands — shipped about 6.64 million units in Q3, down 2.1% YoY, and held 95.4% of the market, up 2.1 percentage points from Q3 2025. Foreign brands continue to shrink: Samsung exited the China TV market in May 2026, and the remaining three — Sony, Philips and Sharp — shipped under 150,000 units combined in the quarter. Inference: with the overall decline milder than the top-eight decline gap implies, the contraction is concentrated in smaller and foreign-brand accounts, so panel and component order planning, as well as channel inventory allocation, increasingly depends on a narrower set of buyers with greater negotiating leverage.

**「Basis of the figures」** The metric is brand-level set shipments \(sell-in\) for mainland China, not retail sell-through, so channel inventory movements are not captured. The 30.36 million unit full-year figure is a RUNTO forecast, not a reported outcome; only Q3 and nine-month figures are actuals. The 2010-onward comparison is the research firm&\#x27;s own historical series.
