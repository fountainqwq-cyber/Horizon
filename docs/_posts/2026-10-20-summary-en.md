### [Tenstorrent Raises $1.5B at $6.2B Valuation for Open-Weight Inference Chips](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/19/tenstorrent-hits-6-2b-valuation-to-scale-open-weight-ai-chips/) ⭐️ 7.5/10

Tenstorrent, a chip startup focused on AI inference, raised $1.5 billion at a $6.2 billion valuation, more than doubling the $2.6 billion valuation it reportedly reached in late 2024. The round was led by Trace Capital and Innovation X, with Cadence Ventures, Eclipse, Fidelity, SanDisk and T. Rowe Price also participating. CEO Jim Keller told Bloomberg the company chose to stay independent and raise more capital after demonstrating that its Galaxy servers could run DeepSeek models; the money will go toward scaling chips tailored for serving open-weight AI models and video generation.

rss · TechCrunch AI · Oct 19, 18:37

**「Why it matters」** The raise gives Tenstorrent more room to compete for inference workloads against Nvidia, positioning its hardware for companies deploying open-weight models rather than training frontier systems. It follows reported acquisition interest from Intel and Qualcomm at valuations of up to $5 billion about five months earlier, according to the report.

**「Context」** No shipment volumes, customer commitments or delivery dates for the new chips were disclosed, and the funding is not itself evidence of deployed product. TechCrunch also reported that rival AI chip maker Etched was fielding investment offers at valuations of $40 billion or more.

### [Nvidia releases open Nemotron and Bolt models for enterprise and local agents](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/19/nvidia-launches-new-open-models-to-run-agents-in-the-enterprise-and-on-pcs/) ⭐️ 7.5/10

Nvidia launched open-weight Nemotron 3.5 Super VL \(120B parameters\) for enterprise agents and two lightweight Bolt models for local agent workloads on RTX Spark AI PCs and DGX Spark workstations. Nvidia claims Super VL has similar accuracy to its larger Nemotron 3 Ultra on instruction-following and tool-use tasks and, when post-trained for specific tasks, runs faster than named Anthropic and OpenAI models; these are vendor claims. Super VL and Bolt 74B-A9B are available now, with Bolt E4B due later this month.

rss · TechCrunch AI · Oct 19, 15:37

**「Impact」** Nvidia says enterprises can post-train and host Super VL on their own infrastructure, while AI PC users can run agents locally instead of sending workloads to the cloud. Nvidia lists Skydance, EY, Ramp, Crowdstrike, Perplexity and Salesforce as Super VL users; independent benchmarks were not provided.

**「Details」** Bolt 74B-A9B 24GB targets multistep reasoning, tool calling and web browsing; Bolt E4B targets conversational agents. Nvidia points to partner systems from Microsoft and Dell. Source: TechCrunch, https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/19/nvidia-launches-new-open-models-to-run-agents-in-the-enterprise-and-on-pcs/

### [AWS launches TorchNeuron for Trainium](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/19/amazon-is-taking-its-biggest-shot-at-nvidia-yet-with-new-torchneuron-dev-tool/) ⭐️ 7.3/10

Amazon Web Services released TorchNeuron, a PyTorch-compatible tool that AWS says lets PyTorch applications run on its Trainium AI chips after changing one configuration line, without rewriting code. The move targets Nvidia’s CUDA software advantage by lowering the effort to move workloads to Amazon’s custom silicon; AWS also says it will contribute upstream PyTorch changes to reduce chip-specific lock-in. The ease and cost benefits are Amazon claims and were not independently verified in the source.

rss · TechCrunch AI · Oct 19, 20:09

**「Impact」** If it works as described, developers evaluating Trainium could port existing PyTorch workloads with far less engineering work, increasing pressure on Nvidia’s software moat and on Nvidia-linked neoclouds. Practical results may depend on model support and performance beyond AWS’s cited examples.

**「Details」** Announced Oct. 19, 2026. AWS says about 85% of AI apps use PyTorch and that voice-AI provider Deepgram moved to Trainium with native PyTorch support, processing 70% more audio per dollar at production-grade accuracy; those figures are vendor claims. No independent benchmarks were provided in the source.

### [India smartphone shipments drop 26% in 2026Q3 to weakest third quarter since 2016: Omdia](https://www.ithome.com/1/015/194.htm) ⭐️ 8.5/10

Omdia estimated that India smartphone shipments fell 26% year on year in 2026Q3 to about 35.8 million units, the weakest third quarter since 2016, IT Home reported on 20 October, citing Omdia&\#x27;s 19 October report. Samsung returned to first place with about 6.7 million units, ahead of vivo at about 6.4 million, down 34% and the steepest fall among the top five; OPPO shipped about 4.9 million \(-24%\) and Xiaomi about 4.7 million \(-28%\), while Apple was fifth at about 3.9 million. Omdia attributed the decline to higher handset prices, cautious replacement demand, weaker online momentum and conservative channel stocking, with a later Diwali shifting some September offline festive stocking into October. Chinese vendors&\#x27; combined share fell to about 68% from about 75% a year earlier, Omdia estimated. Source: https://www.ithome.com/1/015/194.htm

rss · IT Home industry reports \(strict trend filter\) · Oct 20, 02:43

**「Why it matters」** Evidence: Omdia&\#x27;s vendor data show a 26% market contraction and a change at the top, with vivo&\#x27;s 34% decline the largest among the top five and Chinese vendors&\#x27; combined share down about 7 percentage points. This points to a demand and channel-stocking problem rather than only a single brand&\#x27;s execution issue. Inference: if cautious stocking persists, brands with heavy exposure to India face weaker sell-in visibility and higher inventory discipline requirements, while channel partners gain leverage in negotiations; component suppliers tied to India smartphone volumes would see softer near-term order signals, though the source gives no component-order figures. The reported shift toward channel partnerships, replacement and premiumisation also raises the importance of mix and retail execution over online promotion-led volume.

**「Data caveat」** Methodological note: this is an IT Home summary of an Omdia report, not the primary Omdia release; the figures are shipment estimates, not sell-through. The year-on-year comparison is distorted by the later Diwali, which moved some offline festive stocking from September into October, so the 26% decline is a single-quarter result and does not by itself establish a longer structural downturn.
