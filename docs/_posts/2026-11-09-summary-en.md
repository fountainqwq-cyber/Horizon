### [Nvidia board opens independent probe into CEO Huang&\#x27;s ties to alleged Chinese spy](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/08/nvidia-says-its-investigating-ceo-jensen-huangs-relationship-with-possible-chinese-spy/) ⭐️ 8.5/10

Nvidia&\#x27;s board said Sunday it launched an independent investigation, run by independent directors with outside counsel, into CEO Jensen Huang&\#x27;s relationship with Alice Huang, a former Megaspeed executive whom a memo circulating among U.S. policymakers has described as a possible Chinese espionage recruit. Jensen Huang separately told staff he had an extramarital affair, apologizing to his family, colleagues, board, partners and shareholders, and said he supports and will cooperate with the review. The board said it has &quot;no reason to believe&quot; national-security or company secrets were compromised and expressed confidence in Huang and his leadership team; China&\#x27;s embassy in Washington has dismissed the allegations as groundless, and no one has been charged.

**「Why it matters」** Nvidia sits at the center of the AI chip supply chain, and its board&\#x27;s review touches leadership continuity at a company whose customers and partners depend on its chips, while U.S. export policy toward China already scrutinizes Nvidia&\#x27;s business and its Megaspeed ties.

**「Dates to watch」** Nvidia reports quarterly earnings November 17, and three House chairmen have requested a closed-door briefing from Huang on November 18, according to the report.

### [NVIDIA, Korea&\#x27;s MSIT Expand Sovereign AI Push](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/korea-sovereign-ai-collaboration/) ⭐️ 6.2/10

NVIDIA and South Korea&\#x27;s Ministry of Science and ICT \(MSIT\) said they are expanding their sovereign AI collaboration, covering AI semiconductors, data center infrastructure, talent, startups, models and security. The announcement sets groundwork for Korean NPU and XPU companies to explore participation in NVIDIA&\#x27;s NVLink Fusion ecosystem, and includes NVIDIA training and certification programs, hackathons and technical seminars. NVIDIA also said it will work with three selected consortia and four Korean foundation-model builders under Korea&\#x27;s AI for All initiative, using its Dynamo software for inference optimization and scaling. Separately, NVIDIA announced a collaboration with SK Telecom and Rebellions, supported by MSIT, on sovereign AI infrastructure using NVLink Fusion.

rss · NVIDIA official blog · Nov 9, 05:00

**「Why it matters」** The arrangement links Korean accelerator firms and model builders to NVIDIA&\#x27;s interconnect and inference software, a practical channel for domestic AI hardware to plug into NVIDIA-based data centers. It remains largely exploratory, with no disclosed investment, capacity, volumes or timelines, and no confirmed NPU/XPU participation, according to the vendor announcement.

**「Limitations」** NVIDIA described the NPU/XPU element as an opportunity to explore NVLink Fusion participation, not a committed design win. The SK Telecom and Rebellions collaboration was announced jointly and is supported by MSIT.

## Consumer Electronics Industry Trends

### [Shenzhen&\#x27;s consumer 3D-printing cluster tightens grip on global shipments](https://www.ithome.com/1/024/102.htm) ⭐️ 8.5/10

In H1 2026, Shenzhen&\#x27;s 3D-printing-equipment output rose 58.1% year on year and exports rose 127.5%, accounting for 86% of China&\#x27;s exports of comparable products, according to the Guangdong government&\#x27;s &quot;Guangdong Fabu&quot; account. National Bureau of Statistics data cited in the same report put China&\#x27;s H1 3D-printing-equipment output growth at 48.5%, with 3.62 million 3D printers exported \(+90.2%\) worth RMB 9.611 billion \(+109.3%\). Cailian Press reported that four Shenzhen vendors held about 90% of global consumer 3D-printer shipments; &quot;Guangdong Fabu&quot; also claimed Shenzhen is the world&\#x27;s top consumer 3D-printing city, a leadership claim sourced to local government/media rather than an independent audit.

rss · IT Home industry reports \(strict trend filter\) · Nov 9, 03:28

**「Supply-chain consequence」** Evidence: the reported shipment concentration means global consumer 3D-printer volume depends heavily on a small Shenzhen supplier base. Bambu Lab&\#x27;s planned Guangming &quot;3D Printing Smart Manufacturing HQ Base&quot; is to include core-parts production, materials trial production and whole-machine assembly, with planned annual capacity above 3 million units on about 83,700 sqm of land and 373,000 sqm of gross floor area, 75.3% of it production plants, per IT Home&\#x27;s July report. Inference: that co-location could shorten component qualification and logistics cycles for suppliers while concentrating buyer power in a few OEMs; for brands outside the cluster, the export scale and shipment share raise cost and supply-chain execution pressure.

**「Methodological note」** Limitation: the ~90% shipment share is attributed to Cailian Press and a 3D-printing resource-library founder, not an independently audited market study. The report gives Shenzhen&\#x27;s growth rates and national export values but not Shenzhen&\#x27;s absolute output or export value. Source: https://www.ithome.com/1/024/102.htm
