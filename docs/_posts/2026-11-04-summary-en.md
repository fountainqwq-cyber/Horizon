### [Fal raises $1.3B at $16B valuation as AI inference demand climbs](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/03/ai-inference-startup-fal-doubles-its-valuation-to-16b-as-demand-surges/) ⭐️ 8.1/10

Fal, a San Francisco startup that hosts and runs AI video, image and audio models, raised $1.3 billion in a Series F led by Bessemer Venture Partners, with Nvidia, Sound Ventures and T. Rowe Price participating, TechCrunch reported. The deal values the company at $16 billion, double its $8 billion mark in April, according to Bloomberg. Fal also disclosed a concurrent Series E led by Sequoia, bringing the combined total raised across both rounds to $1.65 billion.

The article notes that gross margins across inference providers remain thin because of compute costs, so scale does not automatically translate into profitability.

**「Details」** Bloomberg reported that Fal surpassed $800 million in annualized revenue earlier this year. The article describes a financing event only; no product, pricing or availability changes were announced. Fal was co-founded in 2021 by Burkay Gur and Gorkem Yurtseven. ### [Anthropic’s Q3 compute costs squeeze margins](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/03/anthropic-is-still-grappling-with-the-economics-of-compute/) ⭐️ 7.6/10

Anthropic’s updated S-1 filing, as reported by TechCrunch, extended results through Sept. 30 and showed revenue of $17 billion, up from $11.5 billion, while quarterly compute costs reached $10 billion. Compute consumed 58% of revenue, up from 48%, including $5 billion bought under costlier short-term commitments.

rss · TechCrunch AI · Nov 3, 17:07

**「Impact」** The disclosure matters for Anthropic’s potential IPO case and for the neocloud suppliers it relies on for flexible capacity. It shows that rapid revenue growth is still accompanied by worsening compute economics as multi-year infrastructure deals scale.

**「Details」** TechCrunch reported that Anthropic has been paying SpaceX $1.25 billion a month for compute, alongside multibillion-dollar deals with Nscale, Akamai and Volta. Anthropic said compute and infrastructure expenses should increase in absolute dollars and remain its largest operating expense for the foreseeable future; it is also pursuing “Anthropic Direct.” Source: https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/03/anthropic-is-still-grappling-with-the-economics-of-compute/

### [Google to Default AI Off for K-12 Students, Bundling Search Access](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/03/google-will-let-schools-disable-its-ai-tools-but-not-without-sacrificing-search/) ⭐️ 7.6/10

Google said it will disable AI tools for K-12 students next year unless school administrators explicitly opt in, affecting Gemini, NotebookLM, Gemini in Classroom and Google Search. Schools that restrict AI entirely will lose Google Search as well, since search and AI are now integrated, Chris Phillips, Google&\#x27;s vice president for education, told The New York Times. Google announced the change alongside a school rollout of Guided Learning, an AI tutoring-style tool.

rss · TechCrunch AI · Nov 3, 16:30

**「Impact」** Districts that want students to keep using Google Search must accept its AI features, a practical constraint given how widely Chromebooks and Google&\#x27;s classroom apps are used. Schools get default-off AI but a binary choice on search.

**「Details」** The change takes effect &quot;next year&quot;; Google said it will share specifics with schools in the coming months before rollout. Phillips said the goal is giving school leaders control over classroom AI use.

### [Winbond Q3 revenue surge and NT$71.36bn fab expansion point to memory capacity build-out](https://www.ithome.com/1/021/963.htm) ⭐️ 8.0/10
