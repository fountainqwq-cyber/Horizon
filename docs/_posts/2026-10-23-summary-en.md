### [Anthropic launches Claude Duet agent app in waitlisted preview](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/22/claude-duet-app-is-anthropics-bet-on-agents/) ⭐️ 8.3/10

Anthropic introduced Claude Duet, an agentic app for professionals and small businesses built on Claude Opus 5.5, TechCrunch reported on October 22. According to Anthropic, Duet builds daily briefings and dashboards, runs background tasks in its own virtual machine, connects to more than 6,000 connectors in the Claude Marketplace, and can make online purchases through Stripe Link or Shopify Shop Pay with user approval.

rss · TechCrunch AI · Oct 22, 13:53

**「Why it matters」** The product places Anthropic in the business-assistant market alongside Meta Muse and OpenAI Dots, though the waitlisted research preview limits immediate access. Anthropic says a new classifier screens messages and, by default, declines requests for credit card numbers and government IDs; Duet will not trade securities or move money between accounts.

**「Availability」** Duet is offered as an iOS and Android app and on the web via Claude.ai for Pro and Max customers in research preview; users must join a waitlist to try it.

### [Microsoft: China-linked hackers used AI agents in ConnectWise breach](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/22/chinese-hackers-spied-on-thousands-of-companies-after-breaching-it-giant-connectwise/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Microsoft says China state-aligned hackers breached ConnectWise&\#x27;s cloud backup service, stole application credentials used for data links between ConnectWise and its customers, and then signed in to customer Microsoft-hosted environments as authorized users to reach email and files. According to Microsoft, AI agents drove attempts against roughly 2,250 organizations at almost 3,000 sign-in attempts per minute. ConnectWise says it has paused access to the cloud backup system, is investigating, and as of Thursday was working to restore service. The attribution and figures are Microsoft&\#x27;s; ConnectWise has not confirmed them in the report.

rss · TechCrunch AI · Oct 22, 16:47

**「Why it matters」** Companies that used ConnectWise&\#x27;s backup service with Microsoft-hosted email and files face a credential-based path into their environments rather than an exploit, and Microsoft says the attempted sign-in rate leaves defenders little time to detect and respond. Microsoft tied the speed to ARTEX, an open-source AI framework it says was also used in a recent compromise of South Korea&\#x27;s financial systems, many of which lacked multi-factor authentication.

**「Key figures」** About 2,250 organizations affected and nearly 3,000 attempted sign-ins per minute, per Microsoft. ConnectWise&\#x27;s cloud backup service remains paused while the company investigates and restores it. Reported Oct. 22, 2026.

### [Uber Expands ChatGPT Work to All 30,000 Employees](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/22/uber-puts-all-30000-employees-on-chatgpt-work/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Uber told TechCrunch that its full global workforce of roughly 30,000 people now has access to ChatGPT Work, an enterprise-focused AI agent OpenAI introduced in July. The rollout follows a 1,000-employee pilot this year spanning finance, operations and product, in which about 80% of early adopters were non-engineers, according to Sumanth Sukumar, Uber&\#x27;s VP of engineering. Uber did not disclose spending on the expansion.

rss · TechCrunch AI · Oct 22, 16:00

**「Why it matters」** The move makes Uber one of the larger named corporate deployments of OpenAI&\#x27;s enterprise product as it competes with Anthropic for business customers. Reported pilot uses include analyzing operational data, localizing training materials and building dashboards — tasks outside engineering.

**「Details」** Sukumar said Uber will track how employees use the tool, which workflows they develop and how it affects their effectiveness, then use those findings to guide broader AI deployment. Uber said OpenAI worked closely on integration. No per-seat pricing, contract value or completion timeline was disclosed.

### [Kazakhstan Plans 45,000-GPU NVIDIA Buildout](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/kazakhstan-ai-compute-ecosystem/) ⭐️ 6.5/10

NVIDIA said Firebird has secured a U.S. export license for 45,000 NVIDIA Grace Blackwell GPUs in Kazakhstan and is building a first phase with Kazakhtelecom at Data Center Valley, targeting commercial launch in Q3 2027. Kazakhtelecom has already deployed 400 H200 and 480 B300 GPUs in Astana and Almaty, and plans more than 1,000 additional GPUs next year plus more than 1,000 Vera Rubin GPUs in Ekibastuz. A planned second Firebird phase could bring total potential capacity to about 500 MW, the vendor blog said.

rss · NVIDIA official blog · Oct 23, 02:00

**「Impact」** The buildout would give local developers, startups and institutions more access to accelerated computing while deepening Kazakhstan’s reliance on NVIDIA GPU platforms. Much of the announced capacity is still planned, so near-term impact depends on the 2027 first-phase launch and later construction.

**「Details」** Firebird’s first phase is under construction at Data Center Valley; the 45,000-GPU license covers Grace Blackwell. Firebird also plans Firebird Labs Kazakhstan to support startups, research and talent development.

### [India smartphone shipments fall to decade low in Q3 2026 as memory costs bite](https://www.ithome.com/1/016/961.htm) ⭐️ 9.0/10

Counterpoint Research’s monthly India smartphone tracker shows Q3 2026 \(July–September\) shipments fell 20% year on year to a decade low. The firm attributed the decline to record memory prices raising device costs and weak demand, with the sub-INR 20,000 segment down 47% as higher model prices pushed devices into upper tiers. Counterpoint forecasts a roughly 13% contraction for full-year 2026, and reports local-brand share rose from 3% to 15%, with Ai+ shipments up 306% year on year.

rss · IT Home industry reports \(strict trend filter\) · Oct 23, 04:55

**「Impact」** The data point to a supply-chain and portfolio adjustment rather than a simple demand dip: OEMs restrained channel inventory before the festive season and shifted some models toward 4G and lower specifications to contain costs. Samsung’s relatively restrained price increases helped it reclaim first place with 17% share after nine quarters, while vivo took 16%, OPPO 12% and Xiaomi 12%. MediaTek held 45% chipset share, but Unisoc recovered as OEMs optimized entry-tier margins. Inference: competitive pressure is concentrated on vendors’ ability to absorb memory costs and their mid-high-tier mix as the mass-market volume base narrows.

**「Details」** Counterpoint’s quarterly figures are estimates from its monthly India smartphone tracker; the ~13% full-year 2026 contraction is a forecast, not an actual result. The report also said Google’s India shipments grew more than 50% year on year. Source: IT Home, citing Counterpoint Research: https://www.ithome.com/1/016/961.htm
