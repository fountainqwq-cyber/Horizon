### [Meta removes disputed AI-training clause from business terms](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/17/meta-removes-language-that-raised-scraping-and-ai-training-concerns/) ⭐️ 7.0/10

Meta removed a commercial-terms provision that legal experts told Nieman Lab could have let it scrape the websites of businesses with Meta accounts and use that content to train AI models. Meta said the language had been consolidated for ads and business products and that the removal did not change how it uses web crawlers; The Verge reported the provision was removed.

rss · TechCrunch AI · Oct 17, 21:31

**「Impact」** The episode highlights publisher consent and AI-training data rights for businesses that maintain Meta accounts, though Meta’s insistence that crawler behavior is unchanged limits the revision’s practical effect.

**「Details」** The removed language referred to using content from a business account’s “domains, web pages, or URLs” to “provide, develop, improve, research, train, and maintain the integrity of” Meta products. Meta said the crawling terms “only applied to our ads and business products” and that it had clarified this in the terms themselves.

### [Anthropic reports fewer junior technical staff and more meeting time](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/17/anthropic-says-its-workforce-has-become-less-junior-and-is-spending-more-time-in-meetings/) ⭐️ 6.0/10

Anthropic published an internal report saying the share of junior employees in technical departments fell from 21% to 14% since early 2025, while junior technical hires fell from 27% to 9%. The company also said technical teams shrank from 61% to 56% of its workforce and meeting time per employee rose 20% year over year. These are Anthropic&\#x27;s self-reported figures and have not been independently verified.

rss · TechCrunch AI · Oct 17, 20:21

**「Impact」** For employers and entry-level tech workers, the report is a company-specific signal that AI-heavy workflows may reduce junior hiring and shift work toward coordination and review. It does not establish an economy-wide trend.

**「Details」** Anthropic attributed the technical-share decline partly to business expansion and coding tools letting non-technical employees make software. It said meeting time rose 16% for technical contributors, and cited an April 2026 survey in which 27% of employees reported higher productivity from Claude and 20% said their job had shifted to managing Claudes. The report acknowledges more code may not mean better code or business impact.

### [Berkshire Discloses $330M Stake in Bending Spoons](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/17/bending-spoons-wants-to-revive-software-castoffs-berkshire-investing.html) ⭐️ 7.2/10

Berkshire Hathaway disclosed a $330 million stake in Bending Spoons on Wednesday, bringing its total investment in the Milan-based software consolidator to more than $900 million, CNBC reported. Bending Spoons, which listed on Nasdaq in late June/July, has become a buyer of software companies displaced by the AI boom: it agreed in August to acquire Airtable for about $1.3 billion and last month to buy Miro for $1.36 billion. The company said Monday that a &quot;large portion&quot; of Airtable&\#x27;s pre-acquisition staff would leave with separation packages.

rss · CNBC business and economy · Oct 17, 15:11

**「Why it matters」** The Berkshire backing supports a model of buying aging SaaS products, cutting costs and raising prices rather than seeking exits, a contrast with private-equity rollups. Customers of acquired tools such as Evernote, Vimeo and Airtable can expect product and pricing changes, and employees further layoffs; whether the model works remains unproven.

**「Figures」** Bending Spoons&\#x27; stock rose 41% on the disclosure before giving back some gains, closing Friday at $47.07 with a market cap of about $30 billion. In its first earnings report as a public company in August, revenue more than doubled year over year to over $700 million, almost entirely from acquisitions, while net income rose 171% to $177 million. The company raised about $1.7 billion at $29 a share in its IPO.
