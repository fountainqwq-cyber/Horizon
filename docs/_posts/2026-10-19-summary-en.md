### [Anthropic pledges 5% of shares to public benefit fund](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/18/anthropic-says-its-committing-5-of-its-shares-to-a-public-benefit-fund/) ⭐️ 7.0/10

Anthropic said on Oct. 18, 2026 that it will commit 5% of its shares to a newly created public benefit fund, structured as a wholly owned entity, with an option to add 2% for every 50% increase in company value. The company described broad possible uses, including donations to nonprofits or direct distributions to specific populations, but deferred details on the fund’s structure and governance.

rss · TechCrunch AI · Oct 18, 20:37

**「Impact」** The pledge could give Anthropic a flexible vehicle for societal-benefit spending beyond its $200 million labor-market study. Its practical reach is unclear because the company has not yet specified who will decide which projects are funded or how the fund will be governed.

**「Details」** Anthropic said it will share more information about the fund’s structure and governance in the coming months. It said the fund will have flexibility to pursue “bolder approaches” given the pace of AI progress.
