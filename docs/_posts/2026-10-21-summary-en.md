### [SpaceX reports AI unit at 52% of Q3 revenue](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/20/spacexs-ai-business-is-beating-expectations/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

SpaceX reported that its AI segment—renamed Super Intelligence and including Grok, Cursor and a neocloud business—generated $7.5 billion in third-quarter revenue, or 52% of its $14.6 billion total. The segment posted $431 million in profit; SpaceX said profit would have been higher without $1.2 billion in one-time stock-based compensation tied to the Cursor acquisition. SpaceX reported $4.4 billion in quarterly net income, but still had a $413 million net loss for the first nine months.

rss · TechCrunch AI · Oct 20, 20:40

**「Impact」** The figures show AI infrastructure and agent products becoming a material part of SpaceX’s business, alongside its space operations. That matters to cloud, coding-tool and AI-infrastructure customers and competitors as SpaceX builds out 1.7 GW of compute and heavy capital spending.

**「Details」** SpaceX cited $19.6 billion in total capital expenditures and said fourth-quarter capital investment would be $35 billion–$40 billion. Elon Musk predicted annual recurring revenue of $500 billion–$1 trillion by the end of 2027; CFO Bret Johnsen forecast first-quarter 2027 revenue of $33 billion–$37 billion. Musk said the next Starship test flight, expected in November, would attempt to recover both stages. Source: https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/20/spacexs-ai-business-is-beating-expectations/

### [Google Releases TorchTPU for PyTorch on TPUs](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/20/google-has-joined-amazon-in-attacking-nvidias-stronghold-with-ai-app-developers/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Google released TorchTPU as an open-source project on Oct. 20, 2026, saying existing PyTorch AI apps can move to its Tensor Processing Units and Google Cloud through a configuration change without rewriting, TechCrunch reported. The release came one day after Amazon’s similar TorchNeuron tool for Trainium chips and AWS.

rss · TechCrunch AI · Oct 20, 18:38

**「Impact」** The move gives PyTorch developers another lower-effort migration path to non-Nvidia hardware, specifically Google Cloud TPUs, alongside Amazon’s AWS/Trainium option. Google says it worked with Meta, Hugging Face, Inferact \(vLLM\), and RadixArk \(SGLang\) on compatibility.

**「Details」** TorchTPU is available as open source. Google also launched a TPU agent suite to help migrate apps and fix errors, and updated LiteRT for PyTorch apps on phones, laptops, and embedded devices. Amazon said it would submit upstream PyTorch code to decouple features from Nvidia chips. No independent adoption figures were disclosed. Source: https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/20/google-has-joined-amazon-in-attacking-nvidias-stronghold-with-ai-app-developers/

### [Meta&\#x27;s Muse passes 10M US downloads in 40 days](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/20/metas-muse-hits-10m-u-s-downloads-in-40-days-a-milestone-reached-by-only-11-other-apps/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Meta&\#x27;s AI companion app Muse drew about 10.9 million downloads in its first 40 days after a September launch, including 10.2 million in the U.S., according to Sensor Tower estimates reported by TechCrunch. Sensor Tower analyst Kara Lee said only 11 other apps have reached 10 million U.S. downloads within 40 days, the last being Meta&\#x27;s Threads in July 2023. The figures are third-party estimates; Meta has not disclosed them officially.

rss · TechCrunch AI · Oct 20, 17:12

**「Impact」** The numbers suggest Meta&\#x27;s cross-app promotion and heavier advertising are giving Muse an unusually fast start against ChatGPT, Claude and Grok in the AI companion market. Sensor Tower also reports better early retention for Muse, though single-estimator data leaves the durability of that lead uncertain.

**「Key figures」** Sensor Tower put Muse&\#x27;s U.S. 7-day retention at 30% and 14-day retention at 23%, versus a 19% and 16% average for rivals. Daily active users reached 2.5 million on October 16, up 15% week over week. Meta allocated 39% of its October ad spend to Muse, up from 23% in September. Muse remains available only in the U.S. and Canada.

### [China handset shipments fall 21.7% in September 2026, CAICT reports](https://www.ithome.com/1/015/666.htm) ⭐️ 8.2/10

CAICT&\#x27;s September 2026 China handset market report, cited by IT Home, put domestic shipments at 21.856 million units, down 21.7% year over year; 5G models totaled 19.234 million, down 20.2% and 88.0% of shipments. For January–September 2026, shipments were 189 million units, down 14.1%, with 5G at 171 million \(-8.7%\) and 90.6% share. September smartphone shipments were 19.739 million \(-22.9%\) and domestic-brand shipments 18.203 million \(-23.0%, 83.3% share\), according to the same release. Source: https://www.ithome.com/1/015/666.htm

rss · IT Home industry reports \(strict trend filter\) · Oct 21, 01:34

**「Impact」** CAICT&\#x27;s figures show a lower market-wide shipment base in September and January–September 2026, with domestic-brand volumes \(-23.0%\) and smartphones \(-22.9%\) falling faster than total handsets \(-21.7%\). The concrete consequence supported by the release is weaker shipment volume for brands and their manufacturing/component supply chains; the report does not provide channel inventory, ASPs, vendor-level shares or orders, so inventory and supplier effects cannot be quantified. Inference: this is a broad market contraction rather than a single-product event, but CAICT offers no causal analysis.

**「Details」** Methodological limitation: the release reports shipment volumes and new-model counts, not consumer sell-through. It also shows September new-model launches at 45 \(-4.3%\), with 5G models at 33 \(+43.5%, 73.3% of launches\); January–September new models were 286 \(-28.1%\).
