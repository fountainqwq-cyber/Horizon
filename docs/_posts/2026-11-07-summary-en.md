### [Ramp: enterprise AI token-spend growth slows sharply](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/06/ramp-data-shows-ai-spending-stalling-just-as-anthropic-nears-its-ipo/) ⭐️ 7.6/10

Ramp customer-panel data reported by TechCrunch shows enterprise AI token spend grew just 1.5% across August and September, down from 42% month-over-month in July, even as usage volumes rose. Ramp lead economist Ara Kharazian attributes the flattening to vendor price cuts, enterprise discounts, competition from cheap and open models, and buyers becoming more cost-conscious. TechCrunch frames the figures as a caution for AI vendors including Anthropic as it gears up for an IPO, though the report gives no IPO date or filing details.

rss · TechCrunch AI · Nov 6, 15:54

**「Impact」** Slower token-spend growth could pressure revenue trajectories at OpenAI, Anthropic and other model providers even if enterprise usage keeps rising. For enterprise buyers, the pricing competition and discounts described by Ramp imply more leverage and lower costs.

**「Details」** Ramp&\#x27;s data showed a partial October rebound: token spend rose 8.9% from September 23 to October 23, which Kharazian said did not remove strong headwinds. The figures come from Ramp&\#x27;s customer data rather than a broad market measure.

### [Crusoe sells unfinished Wyoming data center to Google](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/06/crusoe-sells-troubled-wyoming-data-center-project-to-google/) ⭐️ 7.5/10

Crusoe confirmed it sold its unfinished southeastern Wyoming data center project to Google. Google, which Crusoe had previously tried to land as a customer, is taking over development after reportedly raising concerns about cost and timeline under Crusoe, and plans to invest $17 billion in the campus. The purchase price was not disclosed.

rss · TechCrunch AI · Nov 7, 01:40

**「Impact」** The handover highlights execution risk for independent AI data-center developers and gives Google direct control of a large Wyoming campus. Crusoe is still developing a separate Google data center near Amarillo, Texas.

**「Details」** Crusoe originally announced the project with Tallgrass and planned to scale it to 10 gigawatts. Google did not disclose how much it paid. Source article: https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/06/crusoe-sells-troubled-wyoming-data-center-project-to-google/

### [CoreWeave 2032 Bond Yield Tops 16% on AI Credit Stress](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/06/coreweave-bond-yield-tops-16percent-as-ai-credit-stress-rises.html) ⭐️ 7.5/10

Debt investors are charging AI data-center builder CoreWeave more: its 2032 bond yield has climbed above 16%, while Deutsche Bank said its five-year credit default swaps widened above October’s peak before easing. Goldman Sachs estimates possible US data-center moratoriums could affect 45% of planned capacity, mostly in Texas; the Trump administration still expects growth as forecast.

rss · CNBC business and economy · Nov 6, 17:09

**「Impact」** Higher yields and CDS raise financing costs for neoclouds such as CoreWeave and for large AI-linked borrowers including Nvidia, Broadcom and Amazon, whose CDS have risen sharply from low levels. A capex retrenchment could mean fewer or shorter data-center compute contracts and delayed prepayments.

**「Details」** CoreWeave’s 2032 bond carries a 9.625% coupon; its five-year CDS reached above 1,289 basis points on Thursday and traded around 1,155 on Friday, per Deutsche Bank. Goldman counted $296 billion of hyperscaler debt issuance this year and forecasts $440 billion next year plus $300 billion of project-level issuance; BofA expects AI-related credit issuance to reach $685 billion in 2027. Source: CNBC \(https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/06/coreweave-bond-yield-tops-16percent-as-ai-credit-stress-rises.html\).

### [Daly Signals Further Fed Rate Hikes as Inflation Persists](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/06/fed-daly-interest-rates-inflation.html) ⭐️ 6.5/10

San Francisco Fed President Mary Daly said on Nov. 6 that further interest-rate increases are likely, though the timing and size remain undecided. Her comments followed an October jobs report showing payrolls rose 165,000 and unemployment fell to 4.1%, while average hourly earnings grew 3.1% year over year—below the 3.7% September CPI inflation rate.

rss · CNBC business and economy · Nov 6, 19:05

**「Impact」** The signal matters for U.S. borrowers and rate-sensitive businesses because another hike would keep mortgage, card and business-loan costs elevated. It is forward guidance from one regional Fed president, not an FOMC decision; the committee held rates last week.

**「Details」** The Fed&\#x27;s target range is 3.75%-4% after a quarter-point hike in September. Daly, who will vote on the committee next year, said she favors a gradual approach and that inflation has been high for almost six years.
