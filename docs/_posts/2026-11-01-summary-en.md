### [OpenAI reportedly cuts funding target to $20B](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/31/openai-reportedly-lowers-funding-target-for-new-round/) ⭐️ 7.5/10

OpenAI has reportedly lowered the target for its latest funding round to $20 billion, down from earlier reports of $30 billion, according to Financial Times and Wall Street Journal reporting cited by TechCrunch. The WSJ reported that OpenAI has secured $11 billion so far and that fundraising met a &quot;tepid&quot; response from some large investors. The round is reportedly being led by a group of United Arab Emirates investors; OpenAI has not confirmed the figures.

rss · TechCrunch AI · Oct 31, 16:12

**「Impact」** The reported shortfall bears on OpenAI&\#x27;s capacity to fund AI infrastructure commitments ahead of a planned IPO, and reflects investor wariness about heavy AI capital spending. TechCrunch links the timing to a broader selloff in tech stocks.

**「Details」** Earlier reporting described a $30 billion raise at valuations of $1.4 trillion, later $1.25 trillion. The FT said a first close is expected below $20 billion, with a later retail offering for wealthy individuals potentially bringing the total to the target. TechCrunch said it contacted OpenAI for comment; the figures remain unconfirmed by the company.

### [Judge lets NAACP Clean Air Act suit against xAI proceed](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/31/judge-allows-naacp-lawsuit-over-xai-data-center-pollution-to-move-forward/) ⭐️ 7.0/10

A federal judge denied xAI’s motion to dismiss an NAACP lawsuit accusing the company of installing and operating at least 35 combustion turbines at its Colossus data center near Memphis without required air permits. Judge Debra M. Brown found the plaintiffs had standing under the Clean Air Act, rejected xAI’s argument that the turbines are “mobile sources,” and allowed the claims to proceed. The allegations have not been finally resolved.

rss · TechCrunch AI · Oct 31, 21:17

**「Impact」** The ruling keeps a citizen-enforcement challenge alive over the power infrastructure behind a major AI data center, a practical regulatory risk for operators relying on on-site gas generation. It is site-specific and is not a final liability finding.

**「Details」** The NAACP is represented by the Southern Environmental Law Center. The article says a similar motion to dismiss from the Department of Justice was denied earlier in October.

**Tags**: `#xAI`, `#data centers`, `#environmental litigation`, `#Clean Air Act`, `#AI regulation`
