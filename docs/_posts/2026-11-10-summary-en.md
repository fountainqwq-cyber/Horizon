### [Meta releases Muse Ember, a low-cost multimodal agent model](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/09/meta-releases-muse-ember-a-new-lightweight-model-for-agent-execution/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Meta Superintelligence Lab released Muse Ember on Nov. 9, 2026, describing it as its lightest and most cost-efficient model yet for high-volume agent work such as tool calls, coding subtasks, routing, extraction and long-document parsing. Meta priced Ember at 10 cents per million input tokens and said it accepts text, images and audio. The company claims Ember is cheaper and smarter than Anthropic&\#x27;s Haiku and OpenAI&\#x27;s Luna, citing an Artificial Analysis composite benchmark.

rss · TechCrunch AI · Nov 9, 18:00

**「Impact」** For developers building agentic workflows, Ember is positioned as a lower-cost execution layer that pairs with a larger planner such as Meta&\#x27;s Muse Wonder; Meta&\#x27;s framing is that Wonder plans while Ember handles volume. Its multimodal input support could let customer-support and document-processing agents read screenshots, charts, scanned documents or recordings without a separate model.

**「Details」** Meta says Ember is the fourth model under its Muse brand, following Spark in April, open-source Glimmer in August and the Wonder frontier model in October. The Muse Agent consumer product launched in September.

### [Otter Adds Multilingual, Code-Switching Transcription](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/09/otter-ai-can-now-follow-conversations-across-30-languages/) ⭐️ 7.6/10

Otter.ai announced a multilingual transcription feature designed to follow speakers switching languages mid-meeting, plus its largest language expansion: 27 new languages, for 31 total once complete. Otter says 13 new languages are available now and 14 are coming soon; the code-switching feature is described as upcoming.

rss · TechCrunch AI · Nov 10, 07:00

**「Impact」** International teams using Otter for meeting notes could eventually transcribe mixed-language calls without changing settings, but only 13 of the 27 new languages are live and the cross-language feature has no stated release date. Immediate benefit is therefore limited for global teams evaluating meeting-AI tools.

**「Details」** Live now: Mandarin Chinese, Korean, German, Italian, Cantonese, Portuguese, Russian, Indonesian, Malay, Dutch, Turkish, Vietnamese and Polish. Coming soon: Swedish, Danish, Finnish, Arabic, Hindi, Thai, Hebrew, Norwegian, Greek, Macedonian, Persian, Czech, Hungarian and Romanian. Otter says these run on its own speech models, trained on public datasets with native-speaking volunteers reviewing transcripts. Source: https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/09/otter-ai-can-now-follow-conversations-across-30-languages/

### [Nvidia sued over Huang disclosures as shares fall 4%](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/09/the-race-is-on-to-sue-nvidia/) ⭐️ 7.6/10

Nvidia shares fell $7.97, or 4.27%, on Monday, the first trading day after the board said it would investigate CEO Jensen Huang over his relationship with Alice Huang, an executive U.S. intelligence reportedly believed was spying for China. Rosen Law Firm filed a securities class action in the Northern District of California on behalf of retail investor Mark Kistenmacher, naming Nvidia and Huang; at least four firms, including Schall, Brown &amp; Schwartz and Levi &amp; Korsinsky, announced investor investigations the same day. The espionage allegation and the case&\#x27;s merits remain unconfirmed.

rss · TechCrunch AI · Nov 9, 22:23

**「Why it matters」** The suit targets Nvidia&\#x27;s public statements about its Megaspeed dealings rather than Huang&\#x27;s private conduct, creating potential settlement exposure for one of the world&\#x27;s most valuable companies and governance questions for its board. The proposed class covers buyers of Nvidia securities from Oct. 9, 2025 through Nov. 8, 2026.

**「Details」** The complaint cites a spokesperson&\#x27;s statement that Huang&\#x27;s interactions with Alice Huang were &quot;not unusual or unexpected,&quot; an earlier company claim of &quot;no evidence of diversion&quot; of chips, and Huang&\#x27;s Oct. 30 comment to Bloomberg that Megaspeed dealings were &quot;normal and not unusual.&quot; Under federal securities law, the first filed complaint triggers public notice, after which investors have 60 days to seek lead plaintiff status. Nvidia&\#x27;s board said it has no reason to believe national security or company secrets were compromised.
